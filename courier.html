Your guide: delivery partner account

Pick up parcels from sellers close to you and bring them to customers.

What you can do

  • Switch I am available on, so sellers can choose you, and keep your location up to date.
  • Accept a delivery request, pick the parcel up from the seller and press I picked up the parcel, then Delivered.
  • Collect the cash for cash-on-delivery orders and give it to the seller.
  • Set your own delivery price for each speed in My details & location, My delivery prices.
  • See your assigned orders and your Delivery history.

Avoid these mistakes

  • One account is either a seller or a delivery partner, not both. Use another email for the other job. (Supervisors may do both.)
  • Keep your location up to date, so sellers can choose the partner closest to them.
  • If something does not work, press Ctrl+F5 once, then write to the owner from the Messages button.
