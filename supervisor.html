Your guide: supervisor account

A supervisor watches the marketplace and brings in new sellers and delivery partners. This account is given by the store owner, on the proposal of a dean: you cannot apply for it.

What you can do

  • Watch sales, orders and invoices, the delivery partners, and every seller's debt.
  • Invite sellers and delivery partners with your personal links (send them by WhatsApp). Their dean (or the owner) approves each one.
  • Collect the site's fee from your delivery partners and keep your 15 % share. You pass the rest to your dean.

Avoid these mistakes

  • A supervisor only supervises: this account cannot also be a seller or a delivery partner. Use another email for that.
  • If something does not work, press Ctrl+F5 once, then write to the owner from the Messages button.
