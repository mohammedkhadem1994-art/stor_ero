Staff: sellers, delivery partners

You decide who joins. Approve, reject or suspend each application. Suspending a seller also hides his products. Supervisors can invite people, but only you approve them.

QR codes of the Join us window

One QR code for each kind of account in the Join us window. Each code opens the join page on that kind, so you can put each one in its own advertisement. Save one as a picture (PNG) or as a vector (SVG), or save all of them in one ZIP file.

Customer reviews

The latest reviews of the site and of products. Remove an unfair or abusive one.

Deans

A dean leads a group of supervisors. He proposes supervisors (you approve them) and approves the sellers and delivery partners his supervisors bring in. The site's fee is shared: normally you 60 %, the dean 25 %, the supervisor 15 %. You can give each dean and each supervisor his own percentage in the table of the delivery fee chain. A dean sees only his supervisors and the people under them, never your share. The person must already have an account, and cannot be a seller or a delivery partner.

Supervisors proposed by deans

Supervisors

A supervisor only supervises: he watches sales, deliveries and debts, brings in sellers and delivery partners, and collects the site's fee from the delivery partners (he keeps his own percentage, 15 % unless you change it). He cannot be a seller or a delivery partner. Choose his dean in the list, or leave him directly under you.

Who brought the partners

How many partners each dean and each supervisor brought in, by kind of account. Only numbers: no income and no private data.

Orders & invoices

Features & requirements

One row per kind of account. Press a button to switch it on (green ✓) or off (grey ✕). It is saved at once.

Trusted delivery partners

Requests with nobody above the seller (no supervisor, no dean) wait for you. You can also decide any request.

Craftsmen directory

People who wrote their trade (plumber, builder...). Approve a listing so customers can find it by country, region and city. Every change a person makes waits for you again.

Advertising spaces (rent them)

Seven places on the site can be rented to advertisers. Set the monthly price of each place, then add the ad of each advertiser with its dates: it appears by itself on the first day and disappears after the last day. Every ad shows the badge “Ad”. Views and clicks are counted so you can show the advertiser the result.

Erase data

Erase the data of one account (even your own), or of the whole site. Nothing can be brought back. Every account stays with its login and its role (seller, doctor, barber, dean, supervisor...), and so do the settings (fees, limits, switches), every credit balance and every debt.

Erase every product, order, booking, message and record on the whole site. All the accounts keep their login and their role; the settings stay.

Erasing data keeps the credit (balances and history). To erase the credit, use this separate button and type your own email.

Subscription plans for partners

Customers always use the site for free. Partners (sellers, restaurants, pharmacies, barbers and the others) may subscribe: each plan has a price per month and the number of photos it allows. The photos are the products of a seller or the haircut styles of a barber. When the partner has paid you, switch the plan on for his account below. When it ends, the account goes back to the number of its kind.

Requests for more photos

A partner writes how many more photos he needs. You decide how many to give (you may give less). The number is added to his limit.

How many products each account may upload

First table: the number for every account of a kind. Second table: a different number for one account only (it wins over its kind). Leave a field empty for no limit. A seller who is over his number cannot add products, and his newest extra products are hidden until he deletes some or you raise his number.

One account at a time

Opening of registration

Switch “Registration open” off in the list above to close sign-up. Write here the day and hour it opens: a visitor who tries to join then sees a friendly window with the date and a countdown, and the sign-up opens by itself when the date comes. Leave the date empty to say only “soon”.


Size of a product photo

Every new product photo is made smaller until it weighs about this number. A smaller number keeps the free database lasting longer; a bigger one gives sharper photos. Photos already added stay as they are.

How many new accounts to accept

Enter the most accounts you want of each kind. When the number is reached, that kind closes by itself for new applications (the form says it is full). Leave a field empty for no limit. Pharmacies stay closed until you enter a number. Every account counts, whatever its status, so remove refused applications you do not want counted.

Site fees by kind of account

One fee (0 to 25 %) for each kind of account. A new account pays nothing during its free start (the first months after it was created); after that its kind's fee applies. You can also give any single account its own fee, apart from the others. Seller, restaurant and pharmacy fees are added on top of the price they type; a doctor, barber, travel agency or taxi driver pays a fixed amount for each finished booking: one amount when the person booked in the app, another (for example 0) when the provider added the person himself. You can set a different amount for one clinic or one account in the table below. Raise the fees little by little.

Every account: age, free start and own fee

Site fees: partners → supervisors → site

Each seller (shop, restaurant, pharmacy, doctor, barber…) keeps the site's sales fee himself; the delivery partner never carries it. He pays you directly, or hands it to his supervisor, who presses "I received the debt" (keeping his percentage, normally 15 %), hands the rest to the dean (who keeps his percentage, normally 25 %), and the dean hands you the remaining part. You see everything and can set the percentage of each account, or correct the amounts of one account, in the tables below. Record each step here (a seller without a supervisor pays you directly). Any way of paying is accepted.

Sellers' debts to the site

The seller collects the money himself, so the site's fee on each delivered order is added to his debt. He pays you by bank transfer or by hand and you record it here, or he hands it to his supervisor, who records it. At the debt limit the seller is paused.

Reports from supervisors

Products: monitor, edit or remove

Sellers add the products. Check every listing here: edit one that is inaccurate, hide it, or delete it if it is fake or breaks the publishing rules.

Security: products and orders are stored in Firebase. Only accounts listed in the admins collection can change products or see every order, and this is enforced by the Firestore rules, not by this page. Orders are demo orders until a payment provider is connected.
