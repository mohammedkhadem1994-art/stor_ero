Your guide: seller account

Sell your own products in the store. You receive the orders, accept them and choose the delivery partner closest to you.

What you can do

  • Add products with a photo from your camera, a price and a description.
  • Accept or reject each order, then send a delivery request to a delivery partner near your shop.
  • Receive the money yourself: cash collected by the delivery partner, or a direct transfer to your bank account.
  • Cash on delivery orders are settled through the delivery partner: he gives you your part and owes the site's fee. You only pay the site's commission on orders the customer paid you directly, by bank transfer or by hand. Your balance and its history are in your dashboard.

Setting up your shop

  1. First create a customer account (see above) and verify your email.
  2. Open My account and press Work with us: seller / delivery.
  3. Choose Become a seller and fill in your name, shop name, phone, country, city and address. Press Use my current location so delivery partners can find your shop.
  4. Press Send application. The store owner reviews it. You will see “Pending review”, then “Approved”.
  5. Add your first product in the Products tab.

Avoid these mistakes

  • One account is either a seller or a delivery partner, not both. Use another email for the other job. (Supervisors may do both.)
  • Sellers: keep your debt under €1000, otherwise your shop is paused until you pay the site.
  • If something does not work, press Ctrl+F5 once, then write to the owner from the Messages button.
Your guide: doctor account
  1. Patients choose you on the Doctors page and write their first name, last name, phone and the day they want to come.
  2. You see each request here. Choose the hour the patient must come and press Accept, or press Refuse.
  3. The patient is told at once (notification and on his page).
  4. Use the Notes box on each line for yourself: nobody else sees it.
  5. Press Prescription to write and send an electronic prescription; the patient reads and prints it from his page.
  6. Press Done when the visit is over.
Your guide: barbershop account
  1. Customers choose your shop on the Barbers page, pick a free hour of the day and book. The hour is confirmed at once and can never be given twice.
  2. Open the Schedule board: it shows the whole day, in order.
  3. A customer who comes to the shop without the site: press "+ Add here" on a free hour (or the walk-in form) and write his name and phone.
  4. Press Done when the customer is finished. Use the Notes box for yourself.
  5. In "How customers may pay you" choose freely: cash or site credit.
Your guide: travel agency account
  1. Customers ask for a ticket on the Travel page: where from, where to, the day and the number of passengers.
  2. You see each request here. Write the price and the departure hour, then press Accept, or press Refuse.
  3. The customer is told at once and sees your price and hour on his page. The payment is arranged directly with you.
  4. Press Send ticket to send the e-ticket (reference, seat, instructions); the customer reads and prints it.
  5. Use the Notes box for yourself: nobody else sees it.
Your guide: taxi driver account
  1. Customers ask for a ride on the Taxi page: pick-up place, destination, the day, the hour they want and the passengers.
  2. You see each request here. Write your price and the pick-up hour, then press Accept, or press Refuse.
  3. The customer is told at once and sees your price and hour on his page. He pays you directly.
  4. Press Done when the ride is finished. Use the Notes box for yourself: nobody else sees it.
Your guide: hotel / lodging account
  1. Add each type of room (name, price of one night, how many rooms of this type, guests per room). Hide a type any time with its switch.
  2. Customers choose the days and hours of arrival and departure, then a room type that is free for all the nights. The booking is confirmed at once and the rooms are counted night by night, so you can never be booked twice for the same room.
  3. The customer pays you at the hotel. Press Done when the guest leaves, or cancel a booking if you must: the room becomes free again.
